Automatic reordering
Off by default. When on, a purchase order is placed automatically — no review step — the moment an ingredient hits its reorder level, but only if you already have a supplier whose "Item" field matches that ingredient's name exactly. No matching supplier means no auto-order, full stop; it'll still show up as a suggestion for you to handle by hand instead.
